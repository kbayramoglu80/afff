Shoulder Dislocation: Belirtiler, Tanı ve Tedavi

Written and medically reviewed by: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Mesut Karıksız, Orthopaedics and Traumatology Specialist · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Shoulder dislocation nedir? Shoulder dislocationnda kolun üst kemiğinin başı eklem yuvasından ayrılır ve çevre dokular da etkilenebilir. Belirtileri ani şiddetli ağrı, kolu hareket ettirememe ve omuzda şekil bozukluğudur. First dislocationtan sonra, özellikle genç ve aktif kişilerde tekrarlama riski yüksektir. Tedavi planı tekrarlama durumuna ve eşlik eden hasarlara göre yapılır.

What causes it?

  • Falls, sports injuries, or force applied to the arm
  • Most dislocations occur toward the front of the shoulder
  • Predispositions such as joint laxity may increase recurrence risk

Symptoms

  • Sudden severe shoulder pain
  • Loss of the rounded shoulder contour and visible deformity
  • Inability to move the arm
  • Numbness or tingling in the arm may occur
  • A feeling that the shoulder is slipping or may dislocate in recurrent cases

First aid

If you suspect a shoulder dislocation, support the arm without moving it and seek medical care as soon as possible. Do not try to put the shoulder back in place yourself; this could injure nerves or blood vessels. Go to an emergency department without delay if the hand is numb, changes colour, or feels cold.

How is it diagnosed?

Muayene ve röntgen ile başlanır. MR veya MR artrografi, çıkık sırasında zarar görebilen eklem dudağı (labrum) ve kapsülü gösterir. Kemik kaybından şüpheleniliyorsa BT ile ayrıntılı değerlendirme yapılabilir.

Treatment options

ConditionApproach
First dislocationReduction of the dislocation, a short period in a sling, and physical therapy. Surgery may also be discussed after a first dislocation in selected patients at high risk of recurrence
Recurrent dislocation or a feeling of instabilityArthroscopic repair: the torn labrum and capsule are reattached using a minimally invasive approach
Significant bone lossSurgery involving additional techniques, such as bone transfer, may be considered

Treatment decisions depend on age, activity level, the number of dislocations, bone loss, and associated injuries.

Recovery

First dislocation sonrasında omuz bir süre korunur ve ardından hareket ile kuvvetlendirme çalışmaları başlar. Cerrahi sonrasında omuz askı ile korunur ve rehabilitasyon aşamalı ilerler; spora dönüş genellikle aylar sürer.

Possible risks

  • Recurrent dislocation
  • Shoulder stiffness
  • Nerve or blood vessel injury (rare)
  • General surgical risks such as infection and bleeding

When should you see an orthopaedic specialist?

  • If the shoulder is out of joint: seek emergency care
  • An assessment is needed even if the shoulder went back into place on its own
  • Recurrent dislocations or a feeling that the shoulder is unstable
  • Persistent pain and weakness after a dislocation

Frequently asked questions

Is surgery needed after a first shoulder dislocation?

Most first-time dislocations are treated with reduction and physical therapy. Surgery may also be discussed after an initial dislocation in young, active patients, contact-sport athletes, or those with significant associated damage. The decision is based on examination and imaging.

Can a shoulder dislocation recur?

Recurrence is more likely in young, active people. Age, sport, and associated injuries affect risk. Surgery is often considered for recurrent dislocations.

Can I put my shoulder back in place myself?

No. Trying to reduce it yourself risks nerve and blood vessel injury. Support the arm without moving it and seek medical care.

How is arthroscopic shoulder stabilization performed?

A camera and fine instruments are inserted through small incisions to inspect the shoulder. The torn labrum and capsule are reattached to the bone. Different or additional techniques may be needed if bone loss is present. The appropriate approach depends on the patient.

How long is a sling used after a shoulder dislocation?

The duration depends on treatment, age, and associated injuries. Your doctor will determine how long to use a sling and when to begin movement.

When can I return to sports after a shoulder dislocation?

A gradual return is considered once pain has settled and shoulder movement and strength are adequate. Timing depends on the sport and treatment and is determined during follow-up.
