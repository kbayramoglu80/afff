Ultrasound-Guided Injections: Targeted Treatment Around Joints, Tendons, and Nerves

Written and medically reviewed by: Assoc. Prof. Mesut Kariksiz, Orthopaedic Surgeon · Last updated: November 15, 2026

What is an ultrasound-guided injection? Ultrasound-guided injection, iğnenin eklem, tendon kılıfı, bursa veya sinir çevresindeki hedef noktaya canlı ultrason görüntüsü izlenerek yerleştirildiği bir uygulamadır. Doğru noktaya ilaç verilmesine, çevre damar ve sinirlerin korunmasına yardımcı olur. Kortizon, hiyalüronik asit, PRP gibi farklı maddeler uygulanabilir. Injection ağrıyı azaltmaya yardımcı olabilir; ancak çoğu durumda altta yatan nedeni ortadan kaldırmaz ve her şikâyette uygun değildir.

Why use ultrasound guidance?

  • The needle and medication can be followed in real time, helping guide placement toward the target
  • Nearby blood vessels, nerves, and tendons can be visualized and avoided
  • It does not use radiation, can be repeated, and may be suitable for imaging during pregnancy
  • It can help target deep or difficult-to-access joints, such as the hip or shoulder, and small structures such as tendon sheaths and nerves
  • It may help with diagnosis in some conditions by showing fluid, thickening, or tears

Ultrasound guidance does not guarantee that an injection will work; it helps guide the needle to the intended location. Benefit depends on the diagnosis and the patient.

How is it performed?

  1. Assessment: An examination and, if needed, ultrasound are used to establish a diagnosis and determine whether an injection is appropriate.
  2. Preparation: The skin is cleaned with antiseptic; local anesthesia may be used if needed.
  3. Yönlendirme: The target structure is visualized with an ultrasound probe, and the needle is placed under real-time guidance.
  4. Administration: Medication is injected slowly, and its spread is monitored on ultrasound.
  5. Aftercare: Brief rest; the patient goes home the same day.

What should you expect afterward?

  • İlk saatlerde lokal anesteziye bağlı uyuşma; ardından birkaç gün enjeksiyon yerinde hassasiyet olabilir
  • Corticosteroids usually begin to take effect within days; some people may have a temporary pain flare during the first few days
  • Brief rest and a gradual return to activity are recommended after an injection; follow your physician’s instructions.
  • Physical therapy and exercise are important to support the effects of the injection
  • If the response is inadequate or symptoms recur, other treatments, including surgery, may be considered.

Who may not be a candidate?

  • People with an active infection at the injection site or elsewhere in the body
  • People taking blood thinners or with a bleeding disorder (medical guidance is needed)
  • People allergic to the medication being used
  • Corticosteroids should be used with caution in people with uncontrolled diabetes
  • Corticosteroids require particular caution in areas at risk of tendon rupture

Possible risks

  • Injection yerinde ağrı, morarma, şişlik
  • Enfeksiyon (nadir); eklem içi enfeksiyon ciddi bir komplikasyondur
  • Kortizona bağlı geçici kan şekeri ve tansiyon yükselmesi, yüzde kızarıklık, cilt incelmesi ve renk açılması
  • Tendon zayıflaması veya yırtığı (özellikle tekrarlayan kortizon uygulamasında)
  • Nerve or blood vessel injury (very rare; ultrasound reduces the risk)
  • İlaca alerjik reaksiyon
  • Yeterli yanıt alınamaması

Bu riskler uygun hasta seçimi, görüntüleme eşliğinde doğru teknik ve düzenli kontrollerle azaltılmaya çalışılır. Sizin için geçerli olanlar muayenede ayrıntılı konuşulur.

When should you see an orthopaedic specialist?

  • Joint, tendon, or hand/wrist pain lasting for weeks
  • Numbness, tingling, or weakness
  • Recurrence of symptoms despite a previous injection
  • Fever, marked swelling, redness, and worsening pain after an injection: seek medical attention promptly

Frequently asked questions

Ultrasound-guided injection normal enjeksiyondan farklı mı?

Evet. Ultrason eşliğinde yapılan enjeksiyonda iğne canlı görüntü izlenerek hedefe yönlendirilir. Bu, ilacın doğru noktaya verilme olasılığını artırır ve çevre dokuların korunmasına yardımcı olur.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients tolerate the procedure well. Local anesthesia may be used if needed; pressure or mild pain may be felt during injection. The procedure is usually brief.

Injection etkisi ne kadar sürer?

Süre hastalığa, uygulanan maddeye ve kişiye göre değişir. Kortizon etkisi geçici olabilir; hiyalüronik asit ve PRP için de sonuçlar kişiden kişiye değişir. Injection çoğu durumda nedeni ortadan kaldırmaz.

How many corticosteroid injections can be given?

The number of injections is limited; frequent repetition may damage tendons and tissues. The physician makes the decision based on the condition and response.

What should I do after an injection?

Hekimin verdiği yönergelere uyun: kısa süre dinlenme, aşamalı aktiviteye dönüş ve gerekirse fizik tedavi. Ateş, belirgin şişlik, kızarıklık veya artan ağrı olursa gecikmeden başvurun.

Injection yerine ameliyat gerekebilir mi?

In some cases, yes. Surgery may be considered if injection does not help, symptoms recur, or there is a structural problem (such as a tendon tear, advanced osteoarthritis, or nerve compression).
