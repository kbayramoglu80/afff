Revision Joint Replacement Surgery: Replacing a Hip or Knee Implant

Written and medically reviewed by: Assoc. Prof. Mesut Kariksiz, Orthopaedic Surgeon · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Revizyon protez cerrahisi nedir? Revision joint replacement surgery involves removing part or all of a previously implanted hip or knee prosthesis and replacing it. Reasons may include loosening, wear, infection, dislocation or instability, a fracture around the implant, or persistent pain with an identified cause. It is more complex than the initial replacement and requires detailed assessment and an experienced team.

Why is revision needed?

  • Aseptic loosening: Separation of the implant from the bone (without infection)
  • Enfeksiyon: Infection around the prosthesis (periprosthetic joint infection)
  • Wear and bone loss (osteolysis)
  • Recurrent dislocation or knee instability
  • Fracture around the implant
  • Incorrect implant position or mismatch; persistent pain with an identified cause

Symptoms

  • New or worsening hip/knee pain, or pain that never resolved after replacement
  • Pain when bearing weight, a feeling of looseness, or difficulty walking
  • Swelling, redness, warmth, and fever (possible signs of infection)
  • A feeling that the joint is giving way or dislocating
  • A change in leg length

Seek prompt medical attention for sudden swelling, redness, or fever in a joint with a prosthesis; early diagnosis is important in treating infection.

How is it diagnosed?

Assessment begins with an examination and review of previous surgical records. X-rays (compared with earlier images), blood tests (such as CRP and ESR), and, when needed, joint fluid sampling help evaluate infection. CT, metal-artifact-reduction MRI, or bone scans may be used to assess loosening and bone loss. Revision is not planned until the diagnosis is established.

Recovery

Recovery after revision may take longer than after primary joint replacement. Weight-bearing depends on bone loss and the procedure; physical therapy progresses gradually. Antibiotic treatment may be prolonged when surgery is performed for infection.

Possible risks and expectations

  • Revision surgery has a higher complication risk than primary joint replacement.
  • Recurrent infection or need for further surgery
  • Blood clots, dislocation, or stiffness
  • Functional outcomes may be limited when bone and soft-tissue damage is extensive.
  • Results are more variable than after primary joint replacement; expectations are discussed realistically before surgery.

These risks are managed through appropriate patient selection, preoperative preparation, careful technique, and regular follow-up. Your individual risks will be discussed during your assessment.

When should you see an orthopaedic specialist?

  • Pain that persists or returns after joint replacement
  • Swelling, redness, warmth, or fever around the joint replacement
  • A feeling of looseness when bearing weight or difficulty walking
  • Pain or deformity after a fall

Frequently asked questions

Why does a joint replacement loosen?

The implant may loosen because of bone loss from wear particles, failure to bond to bone, infection, or repeated stress. Identifying the cause is important for treatment planning.

How is an infection of a joint replacement recognized?

Swelling, redness, warmth, pain, and fever may occur; some patients have only persistent pain. Diagnosis is based on blood tests, joint-fluid analysis, and imaging.

Is revision surgery more difficult than primary joint replacement?

Yes. Removing the previous implant, bone loss, and infection can make revision more complex and longer. Detailed planning and an experienced team are therefore important.

How long is recovery after revision surgery?

Recovery time depends on the procedure, bone condition, and general health. It may be longer than after primary replacement; the physician will determine the plan after surgery.

Does an infected joint replacement always need to be removed?

Hayır. Enfeksiyonun zamanı ve tipine göre bazı hastalarda cerrahi temizleme ve antibiyotikle protezin korunması denenebilir; diğerlerinde protez bir veya iki seansta yenilenir. Karar kişiye özel verilir.

How long does an implant last after revision surgery?

Implant durability varies with bone condition, implant type, and patient factors. Outcomes after revision may be more variable than after primary joint replacement.
